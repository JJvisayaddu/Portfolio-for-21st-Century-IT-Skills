### ACT 1: PRESENTATION DESIGN PRINCIPLES

</div>
<div align="justify">
Working from the context and essential details provided by our professor, I was tasked with creating an eye-catching, simple poster that effectively communicates its message. In this activity, I applied key design principles to ensure my output was appealing, well-structured, and easy to read.

*(Act 1: Free Coffee Poster)*

</div>

---

<details>
<summary><b>Explanation</b></summary>

<br>

**What:** A simple and visually appealing poster for a free coffee event.

**Why:** I came up with a coffee-themed concept using warm brown tones and cup illustrations to immediately show what the event was about and catch people's attention.

**How:**

* **Selected Templates & Fonts:** Browsed multiple layout options in Canva to find clear typography and a balanced starting structure.
* **Applied Color Palette:** Set up a warm brown and cream color scheme to naturally reflect the coffee theme.
* **Added Custom Graphics:** Incorporated cup illustrations and custom badge elements ("WHEN?" and "WHERE?") to organize key event details cleanly.
* **Refined Layout Hierarchy:** Arranged text sizes and spacing so the main title grabs attention first and important information is easy to read.

</details>

<details>
<summary style="cursor: pointer;">
  <h4 style="display: inline;">Four Principles</h4>
</summary>

<br>

<p align="left">➤ <strong>CONTRAST:</strong> The poster utilizes strong contrast by placing bold white headlines and bright cream badges against dark brown backgrounds, making the essential details pop and easy to read.</p>

<p align="left">➤ <strong>REPETITION:</strong> The poster uses repetition through consistent font styles and repeated rounded coffee-cup frames, creating a unified and balanced look throughout the design.</p>

<p align="left">➤ <strong>ALIGNMENT:</strong> The poster uses alignment by neatly centering all main text elements and placing key details next to their matching coffee illustrations, creating a clean, structured layout.</p>

<p align="left">➤ <strong>PROXIMITY:</strong> The poster applies proximity by keeping matching information side by side, framing the central layout with large outer coffee cups, and grouping related text and circular graphics so the viewer can quickly process and read the details.</p>

</details>
