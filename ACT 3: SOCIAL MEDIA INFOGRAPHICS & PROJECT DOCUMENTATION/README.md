### ACT 3: SOCIAL MEDIA INFOGRAPHICS & PROJECT DOCUMENTATION

</div>

In this activity, I created two connected outputs: a Project Documentation Report and an Infographic. By applying key lessons from previous topics and carefully planning my content, I ensured the infographic directly reflected the report’s relevant information and highlighted a specific proposed solution rather than covering two unrelated topics. Together, these outputs make it easier to understand the real-world problem I chose and show how practical, technology-driven solutions can be applied effectively.

<details>
<summary style="cursor: pointer;">
  <h4 style="display: inline;">Explanation</h4>
</summary>

<br>

<p align="left"><strong>What:</strong> I created a Project Documentation Report and a supporting One-Page Infographic focused on social media management for mental well-being.</p>

<p align="left"><strong>Why:</strong> I chose this concept to address online toxicity and protect mental health by raising awareness on how people can actively cleanse their feeds.</p>

<p align="left"><strong>How:</strong></p>

<p align="left">➤ <strong>Planning & Connection:</strong> Thoughtfully selected a relevant problem and planned the infographic to support the report as part of its proposed solution directly.</p>
<p align="left">➤ <strong>Reference Guidance:</strong> Utilized the sample project provided by the professor as a structural guide to keep the work organized.</p>
<p align="left">➤ <strong>Template & Graphic Selection:</strong> Explored multiple design templates to select the primary layout, matching font styles, and relevant graphics.</p>
<p align="left">➤ <strong>Applied Design Principles:</strong> Applied key visual techniques from previous lessons to ensure the infographic remained balanced, engaging, and easy to read.</p>

</details>

</summary>

<br>

<p align="left"><strong>What:</strong> I created a One-Page Infographic that serves as a practical, visual guide for managing social media feeds to protect mental well-being.</p>

<p align="left"><strong>Why:</strong> I came up with this concept to address online toxicity by offering a clear, actionable tool that raises awareness on how users can actively cleanse their digital spaces.</p>

<p align="left"><strong>How:</strong></p>

<p align="left">➤ <strong>Concept Planning:</strong> Connected the infographic directly to my project documentation report, using it as a visual application of my proposed solution. Instead of just presenting information about the problem, I designed the infographic to spread active awareness on how to cleanse feeds.</p>
<p align="left">➤ <strong>Design & Customization:</strong> Found a solid template to serve as the main body, then transformed it using my own ideas, colors, and graphic elements. Additionally, I replaced the small background shapes with social media-related action icons and 3D emojis, applying soft purple and blue tones to keep the output calm and easy to read.</p>

</details>
