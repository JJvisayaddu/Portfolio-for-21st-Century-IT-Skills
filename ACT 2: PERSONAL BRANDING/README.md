<details>
<summary style="cursor: pointer;">
  <h4 style="display: inline;">Explanation</h4>
</summary>

<br>

<p align="left"><strong>What:</strong> A complete brand identity kit featuring a logo, tagline, header, color, and typography guide, and a resume header for an ube-based product.</p>

<p align="left"><strong>Why:</strong> To promote and elevate local Filipino food, <em>ube (Dioscorea alata)</em>, to the same popularity as mainstream coffee or matcha shops through a fun brand named Ubelala.</p>

<p align="left"><strong>How:</strong></p>

<p align="left">➤ <strong>Color Basis:</strong> Used the graphic image of the ube tuber in the logo as the main reference for the brand's palette.</p>
<p align="left">➤ <strong>Color Adjustments:</strong> Adjusted the tone, shade, and tint of the ube colors to create a sweet purple and pink palette representing its natural look.</p>
<p align="left">➤ <strong>Brand Symbolism:</strong> Paired custom ube and root graphics to symbolize authentic, farm-grown ingredients and keep the brand grounded in local heritage.</p>
<p align="left">➤ <strong>Template & Font Exploration:</strong> Explored multiple design templates to study logo structures and typography, then modified them with custom ideas to fit the ube product identity.</p>

</details>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor: pointer;">
  <h4 style="display: inline;">Description</h4>
</summary>

<br>

<p align="left">I decided to build this branding kit around <em>ube (Dioscorea alata)</em>, a well-known Filipino favorite. Although the Philippines leads in producing and exporting <em>ube</em>, there are very few shops dedicated entirely to it compared to coffee or matcha. I chose <em>ube</em> because it is one of my favorite foods, and I want to create a strong visual identity that elevates it to a popular trend.</p>

<br>

<p align="left">➤ <strong>Ubelala</strong></p>
<p align="left">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• I deliberately chose Ubelala over the expected name <em>ubelaya</em> to give the brand a deeper, more intentional meaning.</p>
<p align="left">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• "Lala" highlights how the sweetness of ube can instantly cheer you up and brighten your mood.</p>
<p align="left">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• It also plays on the Filipino word <em>alala</em> ("remember"), asking "<em>naalala mo pa ba ang ube?</em>" ("do you still remember ube?").</p>
<p align="left">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• I chose this because people often favor imported trends over local foods, and I want to remind everyone to celebrate and enjoy our own Filipino products like Ube.</p>

<br>

<p align="left">➤ <strong>Life is better in Purple and Sweet</strong></p>
<p align="left">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• This tagline highlights the product's core identity through color and taste.</p>
<p align="left">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• "Purple" represents the iconic color of ube, while "Sweet" refers to its comforting, delicious flavor.</p>
<p align="left">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Together, it conveys the idea that enjoying a sweet, purple ube treat makes everyday life happier and more enjoyable.</p>

<br>

<p align="left">➤ <strong>Roots (header)</strong></p>
<p align="left">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• The root graphics represent <em>ube</em> as a natural root crop and tuber.</p>
<p align="left">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Visually, the roots symbolize authenticity, highlighting that the brand uses real, farm-grown Filipino ube rather than artificial flavorings.</p>
<p align="left">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• They also serve as a subtle visual reminder to stay grounded in our local culture while helping ube grow into a trendy, mainstream product.</p>

</details>

<p align="left">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Some colors were selected using a color picker by adjusting their shades, tones, or tints.</p>

<br>

<img src="https://github.com/JJvisayaddu/Portfolio-for-Prelim-Exam/blob/b20df4509463eeccb932677a42dd4889d25a976e/color%20scheme%20act%202%20description%20(2).PNG" alt="Color picker" width="400" />

<br>

<p align="left">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Gray, Pink, and Light Orange were used as text colors for readability and information.</p>
<p align="left">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• The remaining three colors (Light Neon Pink, Neon Pink, and Pastel Gray Magenta) served as the primary and background colors.</p>

<br>

<p align="left">➤ <strong>Typography</strong></p>
<p align="left">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• <strong>Fredoka (Display)</strong> - Chosen for its rounded, friendly letters that match the sweet, cheerful vibe of the Ubelala brand for big headings.</p>
<p align="left">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• <strong>Poppins (Body)</strong> - Selected because it is a clean, modern font that complements Fredoka and makes long descriptions easy to read.</p>
<p align="left">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• <strong>Space Mono (Accent)</strong> - Picked to add a bit of a formal yet simple look that helps small labels and fine details stand out clearly.</p>
